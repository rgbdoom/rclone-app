Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive through rclone. Data accessed through Google APIs is transferred only between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's own server. No data is collected, stored, sold or shared with any third party.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
